# Falcon Cargo Air: Late Change and No-Show Fee Policy (fictional)

Simplified, fictional fee table for a prototype. Cite it by section, for example "Fee 1.2". The app works out every fee from this table. The agent never calculates, changes or waives a fee.

## 1. Fee table

The charge is the released weight multiplied by the booking's rate per kg. The time that counts is when the forwarder's message was received.

1.1 Notified more than 72 hours before departure: no fee.
1.2 Notified between 72 and 24 hours before departure: 25% of the charge on the released weight.
1.3 Notified less than 24 hours before departure: 50% of the charge on the released weight.
1.4 No-show (nothing tendered by the acceptance cut-off): 100% of the booked charge.
1.5 Where a fee applies, the minimum is USD 50.

## 2. Key accounts

2.1 Key accounts may have different terms in their contract. The table fee is shown as "subject to account manager".

## 3. Waivers

3.1 Only the Cargo Capacity Controller or the account manager can waive or reduce a fee, and they must record the reason.
