# Falcon Cargo Air: Escalation Policy (fictional)

Simplified, fictional policy for a prototype. Cite it by section, for example "Escalation 2.3".

## 1. Who decides

1.1 The **Cargo Capacity Controller** approves every message, release and re-offer.
1.2 The **account manager** handles key accounts and any angry or legal reply.
1.3 The **cargo acceptance team** decides when the cargo itself changes (commodity, dangerous goods, temperature).
1.4 The **senior capacity manager** approves releases above 2,000 kg.

## 2. When to escalate (status: Escalate)

2.1 No reply after the request and two chasers by 24 hours before departure (Space 3.4).
2.2 The reply is still unclear after a chaser.
2.3 The forwarder is angry, uses legal language, or threatens to move their business.
2.4 Someone asks for something outside policy, including internal requests to release space a forwarder has confirmed (Space 3.2).
2.5 Any change to a key account's booking.
2.6 The cargo itself changes, for example it now contains lithium batteries (Rules 7.1).
2.7 A release above 2,000 kg (Space 3.6).

## 3. How to escalate

3.1 State the trigger, who should decide, and what they must decide.
3.2 Leave the booking unchanged until that person decides. Release 0 kg.
3.3 If you are refusing an out-of-policy request, start the Why field with "REFUSED - <section>: <reason>".
